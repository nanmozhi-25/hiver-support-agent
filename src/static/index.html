A 93.50% Accuracy score does NOT mean 93.50% of customer support interactions can safely be automated.

1. Class Imbalance Masking Minority Intent Failures: `general_inquiry` makes up 73.50% of messages. While accuracy is 93.50%, the Macro F1 is 67.38% due to lower recall on rare intents like `damaged_defective`.
2. Vector Retrieval Coverage Bottleneck: High retrieval similarity (>= 0.60) is achieved on only 26.50% of queries.
3. Asymmetry of Failure Costs: A false escalation costs ~$2-$5 in human labor. A false auto-handle sends an incorrect reply to a frustrated customer, causing churn and legal risk.

5 Real Failure Modes Analysis
1. Multi-Intent Blending: "My package says delivered 3 days ago but nothing arrived! I want my money refunded." (Delivery Delay vs Refund Request)
2. Short Message Ambiguity: "help refund" (< 4 words lacking order ID context).
3. Sparse Retrieval Coverage: Cross-region UK vs US gift card rules causing low retrieval similarity (< 0.50).
4. False Escalation on Standard Phrasing: Phrasing variations on password reset queries triggering safety escalation.
5. Keyword Overlap Misclassification: Positive arrival keywords ("arrived on time") overshadowing broken physical item reports ("screen broken").
